Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in tanks stored too cool, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. San Clemente Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across San Clemente and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition and flame sensor faults, high-limit lockouts, leaking tanks, recirculation pumps that stopped, and tankless error-code lockouts.
